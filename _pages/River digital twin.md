River digital twins combine observations, numerical models, and data-driven tools to provide a continuously updated representation of river systems. They can help researchers and practitioners understand changing conditions and test management decisions.

## Research directions

- Integrating sensors, remote sensing, and hydrodynamic models
- Representing river hydraulics and changing channel conditions
- Supporting real-time monitoring and scenario analysis
- Improving flood-risk assessment and operational decisions
