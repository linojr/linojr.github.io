I'm a researcher in environmental hydrodynamics, with hydrodynamic modeling as the central thread running through my work. I'm a native of Kozhikode, Kerala, India, and hold a B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Calicut and an M.Tech in Energy Management from the National Institute of Technology Calicut. During my master's, I was honored to be among the first recipients of the National Renewable Energy Fellowship, awarded by the Government of India.

I earned my Ph.D. in Civil and Environmental Engineering from FAMU-FSU College of Engineering in Florida, where I developed a storm surge forecast framework. I currently work as an IT Consultant at the Coastal Ecosystem Design Studio, Louisiana State University.
